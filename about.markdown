# A little about me

I'm an undergraduate mathematics student interested in continuing to explore mathematics beyond my undergraduate education. My path into mathematics wasn't entirely linear. I switched into the major during my sophomore year of college, and in many ways, it felt like discovering something I hadn't known I was looking for.

What began as an academic change has grown into a desire to keep learning, asking questions, and engaging more deeply with the subject.

## My interests

I'm interested in mathematics both for its ideas and for the experiences people have while learning it.

I enjoy exploring mathematical concepts and developing a deeper understanding of the discipline. I'm also interested in mathematics education, particularly questions surrounding access, opportunity, and belonging in STEM.

Some questions I find myself thinking about include:

- How do students develop confidence and a sense of belonging in mathematics?
- How do educational structures shape who gets opportunities to succeed in STEM?
- What makes a mathematical learning environment engaging and supportive?
- How can we make mathematics more accessible without losing its depth and rigor?

## Looking ahead

I hope to continue studying mathematics at the graduate level and eventually pursue a career as a professor at a liberal arts institution.

I'm drawn to the idea of a career that brings together mathematical study, teaching, mentorship, and intellectual community. I want to keep learning mathematics while helping other students discover what makes the subject meaningful to them.

## Beyond the classroom

I'm also interested in writing about mathematics, education, and the experiences that shape how we encounter academic disciplines.

This website is a space to document some of that learning, share projects and interests, and reflect on the questions I'm still figuring out.
