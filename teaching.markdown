## Teaching Philosophy

I believe that mathematics should be a subject in which students feel encouraged to ask questions, explore ideas, and develop confidence in their own mathematical abilities. Learning mathematics is not simply about arriving at the correct answer, but also about understanding the reasoning, making connections, and becoming comfortable with the process of problem-solving.

I am particularly interested in creating learning environments where students feel that they belong in mathematics, regardless of their previous experiences or level of preparation. I hope to approach teaching with patience, curiosity, and an appreciation for the different ways students learn.

## Teaching Experience

### Teaching Assistant — Mathematics for Non-Majors

As a teaching assistant for Mathematics for Non-Majors, I supported students studying mathematics outside of a traditional mathematics major. This experience has contributed to my interest in making mathematical ideas accessible and meaningful to students with different academic backgrounds.

### Teaching Assistant — Precalculus

As a teaching assistant for Precalculus, I supported students as they developed foundational mathematical skills and worked through mathematical concepts. This experience strengthened my interest in helping students build confidence in mathematics.

## Tutoring Experience

### Mathematics Lab Tutor

Through my work tutoring in the mathematics lab, I have had the opportunity to work with students individually, helping them approach problems, clarify concepts, and develop their understanding of course material.

I value the opportunity to meet students where they are and help them develop the confidence to work through mathematical challenges independently.

## Teaching Interests

I am interested in exploring questions surrounding mathematics education, student confidence, and access and belonging in STEM. As I continue my academic development, I hope to learn more about effective teaching practices and the role that undergraduate mathematics educators play in shaping students' relationships with the subject.
