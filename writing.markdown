I am interested not only in the study of mathematics, but also in the experiences people have while learning it. Who feels that they belong in mathematics? What makes someone begin to see themselves as a mathematician? And how do our experiences in classrooms and academic communities shape the way we understand our own abilities?

These are questions I find myself returning to, both through my experiences as a mathematics student and through my work supporting other students.

This space is for reflections on mathematics, education, belonging, and the experience of learning. Some pieces may begin with personal experiences, while others may explore broader questions about mathematics and the communities built around it.
